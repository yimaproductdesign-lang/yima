# Copywriting guide — Behnood Kavian, English reels

Same voice as `copywriting.md`, written natively in English. The English reel shares the Persian reel's topic, central tension and visual plan, but it is **not a translation**: rewrite each line until it sounds like a sharp strategist talking, not like a Persian sentence in English words.

## Voice
A senior brand strategist and designer: direct, warm, a little dry. Talks to a smart business owner, not to a committee.
- "My take:" or "I think" when it is opinion; "the data says" only when there is data.
- Plain, never shallow. Short Anglo-Saxon words beat Latinate ones (use > utilise, show > demonstrate).

## Before writing (record in `drafts/DATE/copy_review.md`, section EN)
1. Central tension in one sentence (same tension as the Persian reel).
2. Three hooks in English; pick one and say why. Hooks that work in Persian often need a different shape in English.
3. One concrete image (an object, a scene, a moment).

## On screen
- 90–125 words over 12–16 scenes; one idea per scene; at most two `*key words*` per scene.
- Headline lines stand alone: a claim or an image, never a label.
- Cut long sentences with a short punch. No three sentences of the same length in a row.
- Sentence case. No title case, no ALL CAPS, no exclamation marks, no emoji.
- Avoid line breaks that leave one word alone; split lines yourself.
- Numbers: one number that changes something.
- Caveats once, briefly, on screen; fully in the caption. The last line lands; never end on an apology.

## Banned
- Naming the UAE, Dubai or any emirate anywhere (screen, caption, hashtags).
- News register: "according to a new report", "studies show that", publication names on screen.
- Tired phrases: "in today's world", "it is worth noting", "game-changer", "unlock", "elevate your brand", "did you know", "level up", "secret sauce", "let's dive in".
- Academic jargon on screen (meta-analysis, effect size, statistically significant): translate into human language.
- Empty questions ("Thoughts?").

## Caption
Hook line · the idea in 2–4 short paragraphs · honest source and limits · one real question · «Write to me.» · last line «— Behnood Kavian».
Hashtags: exactly 4 English tags (2 broad, high-use; 2 specific), relevant to the topic, checked for active use; never a UAE/Dubai tag.

## End card
Logo, «Write to me.», signature «Behnood Kavian» (added by the renderer for `"lang": "en"`).

## Review
Score voice, tension, concreteness, rhythm, fluency /10; rewrite anything below 7 (max three rounds). Read it aloud. Would a sharp strategist say this on a podcast? If it reads like a press release or a translation, it fails.
